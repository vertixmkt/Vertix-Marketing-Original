Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What is the recommended minimum ad investment?

We work with clients starting at $2,000/month in ad spend. Below that, algorithms don't have enough data to learn and optimize efficiently. For consistent results, we recommend between $3,000 and $10,000/month depending on the industry.

How soon will I start seeing results?

In the first week, we already have tracking working and correct data coming in. In the first two weeks, campaigns are already running with the right structure. The first concrete results typically appear between 30 and 60 days — the time the algorithm needs to learn from real data.

Do I need a website or sales page to get started?

Not necessarily. In the Complete System plan, we create the page with you. In other plans, we evaluate what you already have and make optimization recommendations. A poorly structured page can be the biggest limitation on your ROAS — so that's part of our initial diagnosis.

Do I need to sign a long contract?

No. We work with renewable monthly contracts. If at any month you want to pause or cancel, just give 15 days' notice. And in the first 30 days, you have a full guarantee — zero risk for you.

What's the difference between you and other traffic agencies?

Most agencies only manage campaigns — and deliver screenshots of vanity metrics. Vertix looks at the complete system: from tracking (to ensure the data is real) to the page that receives the click. You know exactly where every dollar is going, and can make decisions with real data.

Do you serve any type of business?

We work with e-commerce, digital products, services, clinics, SaaS, and local businesses. What matters is that you have a validated product or service and are willing to invest consistently. If you're not sure if the timing is right, we'll talk before closing anything.
